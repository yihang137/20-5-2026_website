Teen Phone Usage Awareness

Why Are Phones Important?

Phones are an important part of teenagers' daily lives. They help people communicate with family and friends, learn new knowledge, and find useful information quickly.

Positive Effects

  • Help students learn through educational websites and apps.
  • Allow teenagers to stay in touch with family and friends.
  • Provide useful information anytime and anywhere.
  • Help people contact others during emergencies.

Negative Effects

  • Too much screen time may cause eye strain.
  • Using a phone before bed may reduce sleep quality.
  • Long phone usage may increase stress and anxiety.
  • Spending too much time on phones may reduce physical activity.
